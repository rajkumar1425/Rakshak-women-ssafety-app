## 🌟 Key Features

* **🚨 1-Tap SOS Emergency Button:** Instantly triggers an emergency alert, fetches your live location, and sends emergency SMS to your trusted contacts within 1 second.
* **📍 Live Location Tracking:** Starts real-time location tracking automatically on the home screen during an emergency.
* **📸 Automatic Media Capture:** Secretly captures photos via camera and records audio simultaneously during distress to gather evidence.
* **📞 Fake Call Escape:** Easily simulate a realistic incoming fake call to get yourself out of unsafe or uncomfortable situations.
* **🔊 Loud Emergency Siren:** Built-in high-decibel siren shortcut to alert people nearby in public places.
* **⚡ Lightweight & Fast:** Optimized performance under 30 MB with no heavy background interruptions.
